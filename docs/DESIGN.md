# Bannerfall design (baseline v0.1)

A slow, competitive idle game for a small group of friends. Each player rules a castle on a shared hex map, grows an economy, trains an army, and pushes territory outward. You check in once a day. The armies keep acting on standing orders while you are away; the active-play moments are sieges, sallies, spells, and timing.

Every number below comes straight from `js/data.js` (tables generated by `node tools/tables.js`). Change the data file, not this doc, then regenerate.

## Season structure

- A season lasts **35 days** (configurable). Everyone starts equal: 60 peasants, 10 militia, 300 gold, 300 food, 150 stone, and level 1 Keep, Housing, Farms, Market, Quarry, Barracks.
- Nobody can be eliminated. Your capital can be **sacked** (looted, two border tiles defect, 48h immunity) but never captured.
- At season end, score = `10 × tiles + total building levels + 3 × techs + 25 × sacks + kills/50`. Then reset with a new map.

Why seasons: idle games snowball. Without a reset, whoever is ahead at week 2 wins and everyone else quits.

## Time model

- The world advances in fixed **5-minute ticks**. Nothing runs on a server loop. Whenever anyone opens the game, their browser simulates forward from the last saved tick to now, then applies their action.
- The simulation is deterministic (seeded RNG in the state), so it does not matter whose browser does the catching up. There is a test that proves advancing in 72 one-hour chunks equals advancing 72 hours at once.
- Standing orders fire every **6 game-hours**. Sieges resolve **hourly**.

## Economy

| Resource | Source | Main sinks |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | Market, peasant tax (0.06/h each), forest/river tiles | Everything, unit upkeep, **tile upkeep** |
| Food | Farms, plains/river tiles | Peasants (0.1/h), soldiers |
| Stone | Quarry, hills tiles | Upgrades, walls, siege engines |
| Mana | Mage tower, ley-line tiles | Mages, spells, arcane research |

**Formulas** (L = building level):

- Production: `base × L × 1.12^(L-1) × labor`.
- Upgrade cost: `base × 1.55^(L-1)`. Build time: `base × 1.45^(L-1)`.
- Cost grows faster than output (1.55 vs about 1.12 × L), so each level pays back more slowly. That is the idle-game slowdown. Late upgrades take 1 to 8 days.
- Storage cap per resource: `1500 × 1.5^(keep-1)` (7.6k at Keep 5, 58k at Keep 10, 438k at Keep 15). It's tuned so the next Keep is always affordable. If you don't check in, you overflow.
- Other buildings may be at most **Keep + 3**. Keep max 20, others max 25.

### Peasants are the core tension

- Peasants grow logistically toward the housing cap (`120 + 60 × L × 1.08^(L-1)`), but only while food is in surplus. Soldiers count against housing.
- Economic buildings need **4 peasants per level** to run at full output (shown as Labor %).
- Training a soldier **consumes a peasant**. Drafting turns peasants into levies instantly; you can release them back to work.
- Every soldier is a lost worker, so a big army starves your economy.
- Running out of food makes peasants and soldiers desert at 2% per hour. Running out of gold makes soldiers desert at 2% per hour.

### Anti-snowball

- **Tile upkeep:** `0.6 × (tiles-1)^1.45` gold/h. 10 tiles cost about 15/h, 30 tiles about 80/h, 60 tiles about 220/h.
- **Supply lines:** armies fight at -5% attack per hex beyond 3 from the capital (floor 55%). The Supply Trains tech halves this.
- **Unrest:** captured tiles produce nothing for 12h.
- **Sack immunity:** 48h after being sacked.

**Counter loop:**

- Pikes deal 3x to cavalry and strike first against charges.
- Horsemen deal 2x to ranged and siege, and take half damage from volleys.
- Knights deal 1.5x to ranged and siege, and 1.2x to infantry.
- Crossbows pierce armor.
- Ballistas deal 3.5x to knights (heavy) and mages.
- Shields soak 3x their share of volley fire.
- Mages deal area damage that scales with how much of the enemy army is infantry.

How to read it:

- Militia are the efficient generalist.
- Specialists win their matchup and lose others.
- Shield walls and mages are support units. They lose 1v1 but change mixed fights: shields screen archers, and ward mages cut incoming volleys up to 40%.
- Muskets are a late-game power spike behind Gunpowder (Keep 10).

## Battles

Field battles run in phases:

1. **Volley, rounds 1-3.** Archers fire every round, crossbows and muskets on rounds 1 and 3, cannons on round 1. Mages in battle stance fire every round. Mages in ward stance shield the army instead.
2. **Charge.** Cavalry hits with a charge multiplier. Pikes counter-strike cavalry only.
3. **Melee, up to 6 rounds.** Ranged units fight at 30-40%.

Damage spreads across enemy unit types by count × a targeting weight. Shields draw volleys; ranged and siege units hide behind the line. Damage is reduced by armor: `10/(10+armor)`, except for pierce.

**Morale:** after each round, a side whose remaining HP falls below its retreat threshold breaks and loses another 10% in the rout. Musket hits add up to 15% morale damage per round. If melee ends without a break, the attacker withdraws.

The defender gets terrain defense (forest +20%, hills +25%, river +10%). Defenders in a castle take half damage while the walls stand.

## Sieges (the active-play core)

1. An army marching on a capital is **visible to the defender** with an ETA as soon as it departs. A Watchtower shows its size; Watchtower 3 shows its makeup.
2. On arrival a siege camp forms. Every hour:
   - Siege engines damage the walls. Trebuchets need 6h to assemble.
   - Towers (`8 × wall level` per hour, +50% with Murder Holes) shoot back, along with any ballistas and archers at home.
3. The assault happens when the walls break, after 24h, or when the attacker orders it early.
4. If the attacker wins the assault, they take 25% of every resource, two border tiles defect, and the castle gets 48h immunity.

Defender options during the window:

- Draft peasants.
- Rush-train troops.
- Cast **Wardstones** (repair 30% and halve siege damage for 12h).
- Cast **Hex of Sloth** on an incoming march (+40% travel time).
- **Sally out:** fight the camp in the open, with no wall bonus for either side.

## Standing orders (doctrine)

Every 6h each player's doctrine can launch an attack with no input. Settings:

- **Target:** expand into neutral land, the weakest neighbor, or a specific rival.
- Keep X% home.
- Send Y% of the rest.
- Retreat threshold.
- Garrison % left on captured tiles.
- Minimum strength ratio.
- Whether it may besiege castles.

Doctrine only targets tiles bordering your land, and it discounts targets it cannot see.

## Map

- Hex map, radius `2 + 1.5 × players` (8 for 4 players, about 217 tiles).
- Capitals sit evenly on a ring at two-thirds of the radius.
- Terrain mix: plains 40%, forest 20%, hills 18%, river 10%, mountain 7% (impassable), ley line 5%.
- Neutral tiles hold barbarian garrisons that scale with `distance^1.35` from the nearest capital, so expansion gets harder the further out you push.
- Vision is 2 hexes around your land, +1 per 3 Watchtower levels, +1 with the Scouts tech. Marching armies reveal their path.

## Known gaps / next steps

- **No cheating protection online.** Any member can write the world state. That's fine among friends. The fix is to move action validation into a Supabase Edge Function.
- **Mid-season joins aren't supported.** Players must join in the lobby before the host starts.
- **Siege dynamics are untested in real play.** The 24h auto-assault, tower damage, and loot numbers need tuning.
- **Planned additions:**
  - Garrisons can't march out of tiles (only the capital launches armies).
  - Units have no veterancy.
  - Players can't trade, ally, or chat.
  - The map has no zoom.
  - There are no push notifications for incoming attacks.
